# Tiny Globe – Short 1 – The Town Full of Twins (script v1)
## 1. Hook
PIN: In this Nigerian town, almost every family has twins. That is fifty twin births in every thousand.
DOCTOR: Impossible. The world average is only twelve.
## 2. Soup
CHIEF: Come in October. More than one thousand pairs of twins join our festival.
PIN: So what is the secret? The town says: the soup.
CHIEF: Okra leaf soup, with yam. Every mother eats it.
DOCTOR: Nonsense. There is no proof. Other towns eat the same food.
## 3. Names
PIN: Meanwhile, twins get fixed names. The first is Taiwo: the one who tastes the world.
TAIWO: I went first.
KEHINDE: I sent him.
## 4. Button
PIN: You see, nobody knows why. Food, genes, or both. But here, twins are four times more common.
PIN: So, who is the elder one?
TAIWO: Me!
KEHINDE: Me!
