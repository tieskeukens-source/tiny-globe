# Tiny Globe map short #1: "These countries have ZERO McDonald's" (~50 s). New format: toy map world + country pins.
# Cast: PIN (host), ICELAND, BOLIVIA, RUSSIA, USA (country pins in flag colours). Map: gold = has McDonald's, grey = none.

## 1 hook (globe spins, then the map from high above)
PIN: Almost half the countries on Earth have zero McDonald's. Can you name one?

## 2 the map turns gold and grey
PIN: Gold means burgers. Grey means nothing. Almost all of Africa is grey!

## 3 Iceland
ICELAND: We had three. Then in two thousand nine, our money crashed, and they all closed.
PIN: Someone bought the very last burger. It's still on display today.
ICELAND: And it still looks almost new!

## 4 Bolivia
BOLIVIA: We tried it for five years. Then we went back to our empanadas.

## 5 Russia
RUSSIA: We had eight hundred and fifty! In twenty twenty-two, they sold them all.

## 6 USA
PIN: And North Korea? Never had a single one.
USA: What?!

## 7 question (whole map)
PIN: Could you live without it? Tell me in the comments!

## Facts (sources)
~120 countries have McDonald's, so roughly 80-90 countries have none (World Population Review; Chowhound; Wikipedia list). Only a handful of African countries have one (Egypt, Morocco, South Africa, Mauritius). Iceland: 3 restaurants closed Oct 2009 after the financial crisis; last burger + fries bought by Hjörtur Smárason, on display (Snotra House) and barely decayed (Wikipedia/Metro, Lonely Planet, Atlas Obscura). Bolivia: 8 restaurants, 1997-2002 ("the hamburger lost to the empanada", BBC via Valora Analitik). Russia: sold all ~850 restaurants in 2022, now Vkusno i tochka (Wikipedia, Al Jazeera). North Korea: never had one.
