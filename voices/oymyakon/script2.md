# Oymyakon short (~50 s). Hook = question. Cast: PIN (host), SARDAANA (Yakut schoolgirl, 9), NIKOLAI (grandpa, Russian accent), TOURIST (visitor)

## 1 hook (dark morning school road, -50 C)
PIN: Would you go to school at minus fifty degrees?
SARDAANA: I go every day!
PIN: This is Oymyakon, in Siberia. The coldest village on Earth where people live all year.

## 2 school rule (school entrance, thermometer)
TOURIST: Wait... the school doesn't close?
SARDAANA: Only when it's colder than minus fifty-two.
PIN: And if you're older than eleven, it has to hit minus fifty-six before you can stay home.

## 3 life (street with running cars, outhouse)
PIN: It's so cold that people leave their cars running all day, or the engine freezes.
NIKOLAI: And the toilet? Outside.
TOURIST: Outside?! At minus fifty?
NIKOLAI: Be quick.

## 4 record and twist (steaming hot spring)
PIN: In nineteen thirty-three, it hit minus sixty-seven point seven degrees. The coldest temperature ever officially measured in the Northern Hemisphere.
PIN: And the funny part? Oymyakon means unfrozen water, after a hot spring nearby.

## 5 button
TOURIST: So... when is summer?
NIKOLAI: You missed it. It was on Tuesday.

## Facts (sources)
School closes for younger pupils only at -52 C, older pupils (11+) at -56 C (Daily Mirror/inkl; Moscow Times 2017; TASS). Record -67.7 C, Feb 1933, coldest officially recorded in the Northern Hemisphere (TASS). Cars kept running / heated garages; outdoor toilets (Culture Trip, AccuWeather/ABC7). Name = "unfrozen water", thermal spring used by reindeer herders (weather.com, Business Insider). "Summer was on Tuesday" = joke.
