# Tiny Globe – Video 2 – Script (v1)
Titel (werk): The Town Where Almost Everyone Has a Twin
Stemmen: PIN (Andrew, host), TAIWO + KEHINDE (twee bijna gelijke stemmen), MAMA (moeder van tweelingen), CHIEF (dorpshoofd), DOCTOR (sceptische arts)
Lengte: ca. 1500 woorden, ca. 8 min

## 1. Hook
PIN: Look at this street. Count the people. Now count them again. Wait. Why is everyone here a pair?
PIN: This is Igbo-Ora, a town in Nigeria. And it has a strange record. Nobody fully understands why.
TAIWO: Hello!
KEHINDE: Hello!
PIN: Whoa. Okay. Both at once. Let me explain this place.

## 2. Waar en hoeveel
PIN: Igbo-Ora is a city of at least two hundred thousand people. It sits about one hundred thirty-five kilometers south of Lagos, in the southwest of Nigeria.
PIN: Around the world, about twelve in every thousand births are twins. Here, studies and hospital records say it is closer to fifty.
PIN: That is about four times more. Hey, you two. Is it true?
TAIWO: In our family, yes.
KEHINDE: In every family.
CHIEF: There is hardly a family here without twins. Even I have lost count!
PIN: Fair. So the town gave itself a name: the Twins Capital of the World.

## 3. Dagelijks leven
PIN: Daily life here has a funny side. Twins wear matching clothes. They walk to school together. And the teachers need a lot of patience.
MAMA: Two lunches. Two uniforms. Two sets of homework. And two voices telling me the same story, one after the other!
KEHINDE: I only told half of it.
TAIWO: I told the other half.
PIN: And nobody gets confused?
MAMA: Everybody gets confused. That is why we have names.

## 4. De namen
PIN: In Yoruba culture, twins are a blessing, and their first names are almost always fixed.
PIN: The first twin born is called Taiwo. It means: the one who tastes the world.
PIN: The second is called Kehinde. It means: the one who came after.
KEHINDE: But here is the secret. The old belief says I am the elder one.
TAIWO: He sent me out first. To check if the world was okay.
KEHINDE: And you reported back?
TAIWO: I said: it is loud, but the food is great.
PIN: And a child born after twins gets a name too: Idowu. A special name for a special place in the family.

## 5. Theorie 1: het eten
PIN: Now, the big question. Why so many twins? Many people in town say: the soup.
CHIEF: Ilasa. Soup made of okra leaves. We eat it with yam and amala, a flour made from cassava. Our women have eaten it for generations.
PIN: So, case closed? Not so fast. Let me ask someone who studies this.
DOCTOR: I wish it were that easy. Fertility experts say there is no proof that this food causes twins.
DOCTOR: And here is a clue. Neighboring towns say they eat the same food, but do not see the same numbers.
PIN: So the soup is a nice story. But it is not a proven answer.

## 6. Theorie 2: genen
PIN: Idea number two: genes. Most twins here are non-identical, which means two eggs were released and both were fertilized.
PIN: And the wider Yoruba region is known for an above-average number of these twins. That points to something in the family line.
DOCTOR: Genes are the stronger idea. But nobody has fully shown it for this town. Science is still working on it.
PIN: So: food, genes, or both. The answer is still open.
TAIWO: We do not mind.
KEHINDE: We like the mystery.

## 7. Het festival
PIN: Every October, Igbo-Ora throws a party for twins. The World Twins Festival.
PIN: In 2022, more than one thousand pairs of twins came. Some traveled from as far as France.
PIN: There is a parade, a talent show, music, and a red carpet for the twins.
MAMA: The Yoruba also honor twins with small carved figures, called Ere Ibeji. They are kept like family members.
PIN: And the organizers, who are twins themselves, have big plans: a world record for the largest gathering of twins, and a mass wedding of twin couples.

## 8. Slot / twist
PIN: So here is what we know. Igbo-Ora has about four times more twins than the world average. We know the names, the soup, and the party.
PIN: What we do not know is the real reason.
PIN: And the funniest part? When I asked the twins who was the elder one…
TAIWO: Me!
KEHINDE: Me!
PIN: …they gave me the same answer at the same time. Next time on Tiny Globe, another place on the map that sounds made up. Subscribe, so you do not miss it.
